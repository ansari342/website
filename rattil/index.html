Rattil

Upload a Quran recitation and get a vertical video for Shorts, Reels or TikTok: each ayah's Arabic appears as it is recited, with its English translation and a simple line animation. You don't need to know which ayahs it is.

      Download

      Arabic: Hafs, Uthmani script, checked against a second copy. English: Saheeh International. Ayah recognition: Tarteel AI's whisper-base-ar-quran, running in your browser (about 100 MB, downloaded the first time only). Your recording is never uploaded.

      Clean audio gives the best timing. A phone recording of a speaker works, but sounds roomier.
